# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

[简体中文](#简体中文) · [English](#english)

## 简体中文

此准则适用于 LumaRecode 的议题、拉取请求及其他项目交流。欢迎不同语言、背景和经验的使用者与贡献者。讨论具体问题，尊重分歧，提供可操作的反馈，并在分享素材前取得许可。

骚扰、歧视、威胁和未经同意公开私人信息均不可接受。遇到问题可私下联系 <lumarecode@miku552.top>，并附上相关讨论链接。维护者会保护报告者的隐私，并可移除违规内容、限制互动或禁止持续违规者参与。

## English

This policy applies to LumaRecode issues, pull requests and other project interactions. We welcome users and contributors of all languages, backgrounds and experience levels. Discuss concrete problems, respect disagreements, offer actionable feedback and obtain permission before sharing media.

Harassment, discrimination, threats and publication of private information without consent are unacceptable. Report concerns privately to <lumarecode@miku552.top> with links to the relevant discussion. Maintainers will protect reporters' privacy and may remove content, limit interactions or block repeated violations.
